About Me

I am a dedicated Student Consultancy and Overseas Education Professional with approximately 4.5 years of experience in student admissions, university coordination, visa processing, documentation, and international education counselling. Throughout my career, I have worked closely with students to understand their academic goals, guide them through admission and visa processes, and provide personalized support throughout their journey.


I currently work as an Onshore-Admissions Executive at The Migration Australia in Lahore, where I coordinate with students, manage admission-related documentation, maintain leads through CRM systems, and follow up with universities regarding student applications. Previously, I worked with Times Consultant Pvt. Ltd. in multiple roles, including Admission Officer, Visa Officer for the UK, USA & Australia, and Student Coordinator. These roles have helped me develop strong expertise in admissions management, visa documentation, student counselling, SOP preparation, application processing, and university coordination.


With a background in Electrical Engineering and professional experience in the overseas education sector, I bring a combination of analytical thinking, communication skills, attention to detail, and student-focused service. My goal is to help students make informed decisions about their international education opportunities and successfully navigate their admission and visa journey.
